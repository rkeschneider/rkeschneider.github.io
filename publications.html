Black Perspectives on American Imperial Expansion, 1898–1902

Master of Arts thesis in Cultural, Social and Political Thought. Conducted original qualitative research synthesizing more than 2,000 historical newspaper articles to examine competing political perspectives and public discourse on American imperialism at the turn of the twentieth century.

2025 Alliance to End Homelessness in the Capital Region

Regional Overview of Permitted Sheltering

A 60-page policy report examining the legal and regulatory frameworks governing temporary overnight sheltering in public spaces across 13 municipalities and 3 electoral areas of the Capital Regional District. References and interprets legislation spanning municipal, regional, provincial, and federal jurisdictions, including relevant case law.

Oh, Canada!

Junior Producer & Episode Artist — exploring climate leadership at the local level, hosted by David Miller.

About the show

Canada is a nation of natural wonder, from the rainforests to the Rocky Mountains. Local climate leaders across the country are making meaningful imps, the federal government has failed to meet climate and emissions targets. However, local climate leaders are making meaningful impact within their own communities; inspiring the Cities 1.5 podcast to take a closer look. Hosted by David Miller — former Mayor of Toronto and Director of the C40 Centre — this six-episode Canadian miniseries brings together mayors, economists, scientists, and community leaders to show what real climate leadership looks like. From wildfire-impacted towns to flood-prone cities, guests share what's at stake, what's working, and what it takes to lead when federal ambition falls short.

For the Oh, Canada! miniseries, I serve as Junior Producer, supporting the full production lifecycle of each episode — from guest research and scriptwriting to production coordination, show notes, and file management. I also create all original episode artwork for the series, developed in collaboration with the producer.

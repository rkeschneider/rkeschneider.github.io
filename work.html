Policy Report  ·  Alliance to End Homelessness in the Capital Region  ·  2025

Regional Overview of Permitted Sheltering

A 60-page multi-jurisdictional policy report examining the legal and regulatory frameworks governing temporary overnight sheltering in public spaces across 13 municipalities and 3 electoral areas of the Capital Regional District. The report analyzes municipal bylaws, provincial statutes—including the BC Park Act, Motor Vehicle Act, and Forest and Range Practices Act—and federal legislation including the Controlled Access Zone Order, alongside relevant case law (Victoria (City) v. Adams; Saanich (District) v. Brett). It identifies inconsistencies between jurisdictions and offers clear recommendations for improving public administration of sheltering rights.

MA Thesis  ·  University of Victoria  ·  2023

"Expansion is too Clean a Name for it": Black Perspectives on the Spanish-Cuban-American and Philippines-American Wars

Original qualitative research synthesizing more than 2,000 historical newspaper articles to examine competing political perspectives and public discourse among Black Americans on U.S. imperialism from 1898 to 1902. Conducted as a racial and gendered analysis of Black media — including autobiographical accounts by Black veterans and the Black press — drawing on intersectionality theory to examine how race, gender, and class shaped responses to American expansion into Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines, and Hawaiʻi. Completed with a concentration in Cultural, Social, and Political Thought (CSPT) at the University of Victoria.

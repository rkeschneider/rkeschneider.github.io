About Me

Research that serves real people

I'm a policy analyst and researcher based in Victoria, BC, with an MA in History from the University of Victoria and a background in International Relations from UBC.

My work sits at the intersection of community need and policy — from a 60-page multi-jurisdictional sheltering report to graduate research synthesizing 2,000+ historical newspaper articles on race and American imperialism.

I'm drawn to equitable, anti-colonial work grounded in direct community engagement. Bilingual in English and French.

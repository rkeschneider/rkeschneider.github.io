About Me

Research that serves real people

I'm a policy analyst and researcher based in Victoria, BC, with an MA in History from the University of Victoria and a background in International Relations from UBC.

My work sits at the intersection of community need and policy frameworks — from a 60-page multi-jurisdictional sheltering report for the Alliance to End Homelessness to graduate research synthesizing 2,000+ historical newspaper articles on American imperialism and race.

I'm drawn to work that is equitable, anti-colonial, and grounded in direct community engagement. Bilingual in English and French.
